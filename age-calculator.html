How Age Is Calculated

Age is calculated using the difference between your Date of Birth and the selected reference date.

The calculator first calculates completed years, then remaining months and days. It also accounts for different month lengths and leap years. 1

Example:
DOB: 15 January 2000
As of: 15 January 2026
Age = 26 years, 0 months, 0 days

You can also choose a future or past reference date to calculate age on a specific date.
