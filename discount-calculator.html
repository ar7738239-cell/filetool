How Discount Works

For a percentage discount:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount% ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example: ₹2,000 with 25% discount:

Discount = ₹2,000 × 25 ÷ 100 = ₹500
Final Price = ₹2,000 − ₹500 = ₹1,500

Important: Two successive discounts are not simply added. For example, 20% followed by 10% gives an effective discount of 28%, not 30%, because the second discount is applied to the already reduced price.
