Large photographs can create very large PDF files. To reduce the PDF size, select Small PDF or Balanced quality and use a lower image resolution such as 1200 or 1600 pixels.

The converter automatically resizes large images before adding them to the PDF. This can significantly reduce the amount of unnecessary image data while keeping the document suitable for normal viewing and sharing.
