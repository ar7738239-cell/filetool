AdiyogiTools JPG to PNG Converter lets you convert JPG images to PNG and PNG images to JPG directly in your browser. You can select multiple images, preview them and download the converted files.

How to convert JPG to PNG?

  1. Upload one or more JPG images.
  2. Select PNG from the conversion format.
  3. Click Convert All.
  4. Download your converted PNG images.

How to convert PNG to JPG?

  1. Upload your PNG image.
  2. Select JPG.
  3. Adjust JPG quality if required.
  4. Click Convert All.
  5. Download the converted JPG image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert JPG to PNG?

Yes. Upload your JPG image, select PNG and click Convert All.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Select PNG as the input image and choose JPG as the output format.

Can I convert multiple images?

Yes. You can select multiple JPG or PNG images and convert them together.

Can I control JPG quality?

Yes. When converting to JPG, you can adjust the quality slider before conversion.

Does converting JPG to PNG improve image quality?

Converting an already compressed JPG to PNG does not restore lost image information. PNG mainly provides a different image format and can preserve lossless image data after conversion.
