Free Online Passport Photo Maker

AdiyogiTools Passport Photo Maker helps you create standard passport-size photos directly in your browser. You can adjust the photo size, zoom, position, rotation and background before downloading.

Available Photo Sizes

  • 3.5 × 4.5 cm
  • 2 × 2 inch
  • 4 × 4 cm
  • Custom dimensions

How to create a passport photo?

  1. Upload your photo.
  2. Select the required passport photo size.
  3. Adjust zoom and position.
  4. Adjust rotation if required.
  5. Choose a suitable background.
  6. Click Create Photo.
  7. Download the generated image.

Is the photo uploaded to a server?

No server upload is required for the photo processing. The image is processed directly in your browser.

Can I create an A4 print sheet?

Yes. Enable the A4 Print Sheet option to arrange multiple copies of your selected passport photo on a printable A4 sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a passport-size photo?

A passport-size photo is a small identification photograph whose required dimensions depend on the country or organization requesting it.

Can I create a 35 × 45 mm photo?

Yes. Select the 3.5 × 4.5 cm option.

Can I create a 2 × 2 inch photo?

Yes. Select the 2 × 2 inch option.

Can I change the background?

Yes. You can select from the available background colors before generating the final photo.

Is this passport photo maker free?

Yes. The tool is designed to create passport-size photos directly in your browser for free.
