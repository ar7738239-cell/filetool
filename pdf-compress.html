Free Online PDF Compressor

AdiyogiTools PDF Compressor lets you reduce the size of PDF documents directly in your browser. Choose a compression level and download the optimized PDF without installing software.

How to compress a PDF?

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Choose Balanced, Strong or Maximum compression.
  3. Select the desired image quality and resolution.
  4. Click Compress PDF.
  5. Check the original and compressed file sizes.
  6. Download the compressed PDF.

Which compression level should I use?

  • Balanced: Best for everyday documents.
  • Strong: Useful when you need a smaller file.
  • Maximum: Best when the smallest possible file is the priority.

Is the PDF uploaded to a server?

The compression process is performed in your browser. Your PDF does not need to be uploaded to an AdiyogiTools server for processing.

Can I compress scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scanned and image-heavy PDFs are particularly suitable for this type of image-based compression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compress a PDF for free?

Yes. AdiyogiTools PDF Compressor is designed for free browser-based PDF compression.

Does PDF compression reduce quality?

Compression can reduce image quality depending on the selected settings. Balanced compression is recommended when you want a good balance between size and clarity.

Can I compress scanned PDFs?

Yes. Image-heavy and scanned PDFs can benefit significantly from image-based compression.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

The compression process is performed locally in the browser in this tool, so the PDF does not need to be sent to an AdiyogiTools processing server.

Will the compressed PDF remain searchable?

This version rebuilds pages as images, so text search and text selection may not remain available in the compressed output.
