Free Online PDF Merger

AdiyogiTools PDF Merge lets you combine multiple PDF documents into a single PDF file directly in your browser. You can change the order of the files before merging.

How to merge PDF files?

  1. Click Choose PDF Files and select two or more PDFs.
  2. Check the order of your PDF files.
  3. Drag a PDF up or down to change its position.
  4. You can also use the arrow buttons to reorder files.
  5. Click Merge PDFs.
  6. Download your combined PDF.

Is PDF merging done online?

The PDF merging process is performed directly in your browser. Your selected PDF files do not need to be uploaded to an AdiyogiTools processing server.

Can I merge more than two PDFs?

Yes. You can select and combine multiple PDF files in one operation, subject to your device's available memory.

Can I change the PDF order?

Yes. Drag and drop the PDF items to change their order, or use the up and down controls beside each file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I merge PDF files for free?

Yes. AdiyogiTools PDF Merge is designed as a free browser-based PDF merging tool.

How many PDF files can I merge?

You can add multiple PDF files. The practical limit depends on your device's available browser memory.

Can I reorder PDFs before merging?

Yes. Drag the PDF files into your preferred order or use the arrow controls.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

The merging process is performed locally in your browser, so the PDFs do not need to be uploaded to an AdiyogiTools processing server.
