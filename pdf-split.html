Free Online PDF Splitter

AdiyogiTools PDF Split lets you extract selected pages, split every page into individual PDFs, or divide a large PDF into smaller groups. Processing happens directly in your browser.

How to split a PDF?

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Select a split method.
  3. Enter the required page range or chunk size.
  4. Click Split PDF.
  5. Download the generated file or ZIP.

Can I extract specific pages?

Yes. Use Extract Pages and enter the start and end page numbers.

Can I split every page?

Yes. Every Page creates one PDF for each page.

Can I split a PDF into groups?

Yes. Split Into Chunks lets you specify how many pages should be included in each PDF.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No server upload is required for processing. The PDF is processed directly in your browser.
