Free Online Signature Resize Tool

Resize and compress signature images to the required file size and dimensions directly inside your browser.

How to resize a signature to 20 KB?

  1. Upload your signature image.
  2. Enter 20 KB in Target File Size.
  3. Select the required dimensions.
  4. Choose Close Target.
  5. Click Resize Signature.
  6. Download the result.

Is my signature uploaded?

No. Image processing is performed directly in your browser.

What does Maximum KB mean?

Maximum KB mode ensures that the generated file stays at or below the target size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize a signature to 20 KB?

Yes. Enter 20 KB as the target and use Close Target mode.

Can I resize a signature to 30 KB?

Yes. Enter 30 KB and click Resize Signature. The tool will try to generate a file close to the requested size.

Can I resize a signature to a specific dimension?

Yes. You can enter your own width and height in pixels.

Is the tool free?
