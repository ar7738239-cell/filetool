Simple Interest Formula

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

P = Principal amount
R = Annual interest rate
T = Time in years

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Example: ₹10,000 at 8% per year for 3 years:
SI = ₹2,400
Total Amount = ₹12,400
