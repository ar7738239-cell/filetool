Free Online Watermark Maker

How to Add a Watermark to an Image

  1. Upload your image.
  2. Enter your watermark text and tap Add Text Watermark.
  3. Upload a logo if you want to add a logo watermark.
  4. Drag the watermark to the desired location.
  5. On mobile, use two fingers to resize and rotate it.
  6. Use the controls to adjust opacity, size, color and style.
  7. Download your watermarked image.
