Free Word to PDF Converter Online

AdiyogiTools Word to PDF Converter lets you convert DOCX Word documents into PDF format directly in your browser. Text, paragraphs, tables and images contained inside supported Word documents can be included in the generated PDF.

Word Documents with Photos

Have a Word document containing both text and photos? No problem. The converter extracts the document content and preserves supported images while creating the PDF. You can also add JPG, JPEG and PNG images separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert Word files containing images?

Yes. Supported DOCX documents can contain both text and images. The images are extracted and included in the PDF.

Can I convert JPG or PNG images to PDF?

Yes. You can select one or multiple JPG, JPEG or PNG images and add them to the generated PDF.

Does it support DOC files?

The tool is designed primarily for DOCX. Old binary DOC files are not reliably processed by the browser-based Mammoth converter. Converting the document to DOCX first is recommended.

Is this Word to PDF converter free?

Yes. AdiyogiTools provides this tool free of charge with no registration required.

Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The tool is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.
